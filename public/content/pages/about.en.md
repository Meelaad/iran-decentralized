# About IranDAO

IranDAO is a collection of governance blueprints exploring how decentralized digital systems could enable citizen-led governance, transparent public finances, and accountable institutions.

## Mission & Vision

Our mission is to provide a platform for Iranians to explore, compare, and vote on different governance models for a future democratic Iran. We believe in:

- **Transparency**: All governance processes should be visible and auditable
- **Participation**: Every citizen should have a voice in shaping their government
- **Decentralization**: Power should be distributed, not concentrated
- **Innovation**: Leveraging blockchain and DAO technology for civic good

## How It Works

1. **Explore Blueprints**: Review multiple governance models from decentralized to constitutional monarchy
2. **Compare Options**: Side-by-side comparison of different approaches
3. **Cast Your Vote**: Register and vote for your preferred blueprint (18+ age verification)
4. **Participate**: Fork blueprints, propose modifications, engage in discussion

## Roadmap

- **Phase 1 (Current)**: Blueprint exploration and voting platform
- **Phase 2**: Community forums and proposal system
- **Phase 3**: Pilot DAO implementations for specific sectors
- **Phase 4**: Integration with real-world civic organizations
